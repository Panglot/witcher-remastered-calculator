Slots and mutagens

Only slotted skills are active. Each group's mutagen gets one extra copy of its bonus per matching-colour skill (original-game rule, not yet checked in the Remaster). General skills match no mutagen.

    What the data rests on

    Tree layout and connections are read from your in-game screenshots. One-parent unlock is confirmed in-game (a point in Efficiency opened all three skills below it). Lines are treated as two-way; whether a lower skill can open an upper one is untested.

    Skill text is the rank 1 tooltip. Skills marked "Checked in-game" were compared against your screenshots; the rest come from Hack the Minotaur's list, which matched on every skill checked so far. Rank 2 and 3 values are not published anywhere yet.

    Not modelled: the four extra slots from Blood and Wine mutations, slot unlock levels, and mutagen base values.
