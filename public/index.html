Witcher 3 Remastered Build Planner

A free skill calculator for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered, the next-gen edition. Spend skill points in the Combat, Signs, Alchemy and General trees, equip skills in the ability slots, add mutagens for their bonuses, and share the build as a link, a code or a file. Skill sets highlight the skills that share a theme, such as fast attacks, Quen, Adrenaline or bombs.
